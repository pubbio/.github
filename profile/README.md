## Bring your app to life.

Build notifications, chat, live dashboards, and collaborative experiences with **pubb**. Publish events over HTTP and deliver them to connected clients through WebSockets.

| Events | Channels | Presence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publish an event. Update every connected subscriber. | Bring people together in public or private channels. | See who's online and respond when people join or leave. |
